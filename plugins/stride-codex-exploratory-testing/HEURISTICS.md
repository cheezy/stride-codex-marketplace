# Heuristics — quick reference

This is a one-page **pointer**, not the catalog. The canonical, maintained lists
live in the [`heuristics` skill](skills/heuristics/SKILL.md); everything else in
the plugin references those lenses *by name*. When you need the actual entries —
the descriptions, the worked prompts — open that skill. This page just tells you
what's in there so you can pick a lens fast.

## When to reach for a lens

You're mid-session and stuck for the next idea, or you're writing a charter and
want to widen it. Grab a lens below by name, then read its entry in the skill.

## What the `heuristics` skill catalogs

- **General heuristics** — product-agnostic idea generators (e.g. Zero / One / Many,
  Goldilocks, CRUD, Follow the Data, Interrupt, Reverse, Violate Format,
  Beginning / Middle / End). Apply to almost any feature.
- **Web heuristics** — HTTP- and browser-specific probes (e.g. Tamper the URL,
  Back / Forward / Resubmit, Multiple Tabs, Cookies / Session, Special Characters &
  Injection, Resize / Zoom / Responsive).
- **Variable catalog** — the dimensions worth deliberately varying: Count, Position,
  Files & storage, Geography / locale, Format, Size, Depth, Timing, Input method.
- **Tours** — James Whittaker's tours grouped by *tourist district* (Business,
  Historical, Tourist, Entertainment, Hotel, Seedy). Pick the district whose risk
  matters for your charter.

## Related lenses in other skills

- **SFDPOT** (Structure, Function, Data, Platform, Operations, Time) — a coverage
  lens for generating charter *targets*. Lives in the
  [`chartering` skill](skills/chartering/SKILL.md).
- **Consistency oracles** and the **HTSM quality criteria** — for deciding whether
  what you saw is a *bug*. Live in the [`oracles` skill](skills/oracles/SKILL.md).

> These are lenses, not laws. Use the one that fits the risk in front of you and
> ignore the rest.

Tours and SFDPOT / HTSM are the work of James Whittaker and James Bach respectively;
see the [README's Sources & attribution section](README.md#sources--attribution).
